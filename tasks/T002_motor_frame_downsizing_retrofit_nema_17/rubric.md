1. Full credit requires: the correct frame is inferred (NEMA 17) and its four clearance holes are added on the 31 mm square pattern, centered on the ballscrew axis, through the base plate, with both output files written. Major failures: wrong frame inferred, wrong number of holes, holes not through the plate, or a missing output file.
Weight: 20

2. Full credit requires: holes are M3 clearance size (standard medium clearance, about Ø3.4 mm), round, parallel to the motor axis, and fully through the base plate. Major failures: tapped/undersized/oversized holes, blind holes, angled holes, or holes that break into the coupling bore or the plate edge.
Weight: 20

3. Full credit requires: the chosen pattern is the smallest standard frame that clears the Ø38.1 bore (NEMA 17, 31 mm square), centered on the ballscrew axis, with a solid web retained between each hole and the bore; the M3 clearance choice follows from the frame. Major failures: a smaller frame whose holes intrude on the bore, an off-axis or mis-pitched pattern, or a non-standard hole size.
Weight: 20

4. Full credit requires: the existing NEMA 23 holes, the central coupling/pilot bore, the ballscrew bearing seat and retainer holes, the side frame-mount holes, and the overall envelope are all intact; the model stays clean (no stray sliver solids from a careless boolean). Major failures: NEMA 23 holes or bore filled/altered, bearing interface damaged, envelope changed, or the part fragmented.
Weight: 15

5. Full credit requires: the inference chain is sound and explained — knowing the NEMA pattern sizes, checking each against the bore clearance, selecting the smallest that fits, and matching the screw/clearance to that frame; awareness that the holes sit close to the bore (thin wall handled cleanly) and that the bolt pattern (not a pilot) locates the smaller motor. Major failures: arbitrary frame/size choice, or a pattern that would not actually mount the motor coaxially.
Weight: 10

6. Full credit requires: clean CadQuery-only `solution.py` with named parameters (pattern pitch, clearance diameter, hole positions) and a reproducible build. Major failures: hard-coded magic numbers with no intent, non-CadQuery geometry libraries, or code that does not produce the exported STEP.
Weight: 10

7. Full credit requires: evidence the model was inspected (axis, base-plate location, existing holes, bore diameter) before drilling and the result was validated. Major failures: no inspection or validation, or obviously untested output.
Weight: 5
