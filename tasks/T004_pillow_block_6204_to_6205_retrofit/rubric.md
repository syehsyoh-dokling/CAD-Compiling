1. Full credit requires: the housing is retrofitted for a 6205 bearing — the seat is opened to the 6205 outer diameter (Ø52) and runs from the front face to a new shoulder, a DIN 472 groove, a Ø33 shaft passage, a lead-in chamfer and two belt-tension slots are all present, and both output files are written. Major failures: the seat left at Ø47 or sized for the wrong bearing (e.g. 6206, Ø62); any of the five edits missing; a missing output file.
Weight: 20

2. Full credit requires: a single valid solid whose edited features read Ø52.00 seat (±0.04), shoulder at Y = 19.15 (±0.05), Ø55.0 groove (±0.06) from Y = 2.00 to Y = 4.15 (width 2.15), Ø33.0 passage, a 1.0 × 45° chamfer (Ø54 at the front face to Ø52 at Y = 1), and slots 11.0 wide and 23.0 end to end. Major failures: the shoulder placed at 17.0 (bearing width only, ignoring the lip and groove) or left at 14.0; a groove of the wrong diameter or width (e.g. a Ø54 or 1.75 groove from a different ring size); self-intersecting or multi-solid output.
Weight: 20

3. Full credit requires: the axial stack is consistent — 2.00 lip, then the groove whose inner flank is flush with the bearing's front face, then exactly one bearing width (15.00) to the shoulder — and each slot is centred on its old hole (X = ±45, Y = 15) with arc centres 12.0 apart and runs fully through the 15 mm base. Major failures: the groove started at the wrong depth so the ring cannot seat against the bearing; a gap or interference between the bearing and the shoulder; off-centre, blind or round slots.
Weight: 20

4. Full credit requires: the envelope (120 × 30 × 68 from (−60, 0, 0)), base thickness, pedestal, Ø70 boss, all outer faces and the shaft axis are unchanged, and no other material is added or removed (≤ 0.2 % of the volume differs from the reference). Major failures: extra holes or pockets, a resized or moved boss, a changed envelope, or the whole part rebuilt so unrelated faces move.
Weight: 15

5. Full credit requires: the engineering inference is correct and stated — the 6205's dimensions (d 25 × D 52 × B 15) and the DIN 472 groove for a Ø52 bore (d2 = 55.0, m = 2.15) are looked up, the shoulder position is derived from lip + groove width + bearing width, and the passage is checked to clear the 6205 inner ring (Ø25 shaft, Ø33 passage). Major failures: guessed or wrong catalogue values, an unexplained shoulder position, or a passage that does not clear the shaft.
Weight: 10

6. Full credit requires: readable CadQuery with named parameters (bearing OD and width, lip, groove diameter and width, chamfer, slot width and pitch) from which the cut positions are derived; the script regenerates the submitted STEP from the input on its own. Major failures: magic numbers with no intent, rebuilding the part from scratch instead of editing the input, or a script that does not reproduce the STEP.
Weight: 10

7. Full credit requires: evidence the input was inspected (axis, seat diameter and depth, hole positions measured) before editing and the output was checked after (single solid, key dimensions re-measured), with both files at the required paths. Major failures: no inspection or validation, or wrong output paths.
Weight: 5
